How to Play the Imposter Game: Full Rules & Variants

The imposter game (the word-guessing party game all over TikTok, also called "Who is the Spy") takes two minutes to learn and ruins friendships in five. Here's the complete ruleset, plus the variants our own groups keep coming back to.

The basic rules

  1. Gather 3-10 players and one phone. Open the game, pick player count and a category.
  2. Pass the phone around. Each player privately reveals their secret word, memorizes it, hides it, and passes on. Everyone gets the same word - except the imposter(s), who get a slightly different one.
  3. Clue round: Starting from Player 1, each person says one short clue about their word out loud. Never say the word itself. ("It's yellow" - not "It's a banana.")
  4. Discuss. Whose clue didn't fit? Who looked nervous? Who just repeated what someone else said?
  5. Vote. On the count of three, everyone points at a suspect. The player with the most votes is out - reveal whether they were the imposter.

Winning

  • Civilians win if the vote catches the imposter.
  • The imposter wins if they survive the vote. House rule we recommend: a surviving imposter must then guess the civilian word to claim the win - it keeps endgames dramatic.

Popular variants

  • Two imposters (6+ players): Built into the game setup. Chaos doubles; so does the arguing.
  • Speed rounds: 10 seconds per clue, no discussion. Great as a tiebreaker.
  • No-repeat clues: You can't reuse any clue concept already said. Forces everyone off the generic "it's round" tier of hints.
  • Imposter knows: Tell the imposter they're the imposter and give them no word at all - they improvise from pure vibes. Brutal and hilarious.

Tips for civilians

  • Give clues specific enough to prove you know the word, vague enough that the imposter can't steal the answer. "Kids love it at parties" beats "it's a long yellow fruit."
  • Watch for players who clue last and mirror earlier answers.
  • If someone accuses you, don't panic - over-defending looks guiltier than a weak clue.

Tips for the imposter

  • Speak late if you can. Every clue before you is free information.
  • Keep your clue generic but confident. Hesitation is what gets you caught, not vagueness.
  • During discussion, gently steer suspicion onto whoever gave the most specific clue - "that was TOO obvious, like you're covering something."

Common questions

How many players do we need? Three minimum, but 5-8 is the sweet spot. Below four, the vote is too easy to rig; above ten, rounds drag.

What if the imposter guesses the word from clues? That's the game working as intended - a good imposter who pieces it together and clutches the vote deserves the win.
