What makes a good imposter word pair?

The best pairs live in the sweet spot: same category, different specifics. Coffee vs Tea works because both are hot morning drinks - clues like "caffeine" or "mug" fit either word. But Coffee vs Bicycle would be a disaster; the imposter is exposed on the first clue.

Pro tip from many chaotic game nights: pairs where one word is a subset of the other (Cat vs Tiger, Wine vs Champagne) are the hardest, because the imposter's clues sound perfectly reasonable until someone says the wrong detail.

Make your own pairs

Once your group has burned through these, making your own pairs is half the fun. Pick a category everyone knows, then choose two words that would produce identical clues for the first two rounds of hints. If you can describe both words with the same three clues, you've found a great pair.
