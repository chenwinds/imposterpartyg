About ImposterPartyG.com

ImposterPartyG.com is a small independent project built around one simple idea: the best party games need nothing more than one phone and a group of friends.

Our game is a browser version of the viral "imposter" word game (also known as "Who is the Spy") that took over TikTok. Everyone in the group receives the same secret word - except one hidden imposter, who gets a slightly different word and must bluff their way through the round. Describe, discuss, vote, and laugh.

Why We Built It

Most versions of this game lock the fun behind app downloads, sign-ups, or paywalls. We wanted a version you can open in any browser and start playing within a minute - at a party, in a classroom, on a road trip, anywhere.

How the Site Is Supported

The game is and will remain free. The site is supported by advertising served through Google AdSense.
